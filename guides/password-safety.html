Simple habits for stronger online passwords

A few practical habits to protect online accounts

ONLINE SAFETY • 4 MIN READ

Strong account security is less about memorizing complicated strings and more about using unique passwords and safe recovery practices.

Use a unique password for each account

If one service is breached, a reused password can put other accounts at risk. A password manager can help you create and store unique credentials.

Prefer long, unpredictable passwords

Length and unpredictability matter. Avoid names, birthdays, common phrases and predictable substitutions.

Turn on multifactor authentication

Where available, use an authenticator app or security key. Keep recovery codes somewhere secure.

Watch for phishing

Check the domain before entering credentials. Do not follow unexpected login links from messages without verifying the sender.
