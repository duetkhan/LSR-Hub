Small digital shortcuts that save time every day

Organize recurring work with simple digital habits

PRODUCTIVITY • 6 MIN READ

Small improvements to routine tasks often save more time than adopting a complicated new workflow. Start with the tasks you repeat most frequently.

Make recurring tasks repeatable

Keep a checklist for routine publishing, file delivery or reporting. A simple repeatable process reduces forgotten steps.

Use descriptive filenames

Include a date or version when it helps, and avoid names such as final-final-2. Consistent naming makes files easier to find.

Automate the small conversions

Use a tool for word counts, URL encoding, image compression or unit conversion rather than repeating the same manual steps.

Keep the system simple

Choose tools that solve a real problem. Remove steps that do not improve accuracy, clarity or security.
