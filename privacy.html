Privacy Policy

Learn how LSR Hub handles information and browser-based tools.

Last updated: October 2026

LSR Hub provides browser-based tools and informational content. This policy describes the general data practices intended for this static website. It must be updated if new analytics, forms, account features, or advertising services are enabled.

Files and text processed by tools

Tools that run in your browser are designed to process their inputs on your device rather than upload them to an LSR Hub server. Some tools load third-party JavaScript libraries from a content delivery network (CDN); those requests may expose technical information such as your IP address to the CDN provider. Do not use this site to process highly sensitive information.

Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense is planned for this website but is not active until the publisher configures it after account approval. If enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads, subject to the settings and policies applicable to your region. Visitors can learn about Google's advertising practices and controls at Google's advertising technologies page and My Ad Center.

Where legally required, appropriate consent or privacy-choice controls must be implemented before personalized advertising or related storage is used. The site owner must configure any required consent management platform and update this policy to match the final configuration.

Information you provide

This static version does not currently send the contact form to a server. If a working contact form, email service, or analytics is added, explain what information is collected, why it is collected, how long it is kept, and how visitors can request its deletion.

Third-party services

External libraries, hosting, and advertising providers operate under their own privacy policies. LSR Hub does not control their independent processing. Only load services needed for the features you actually provide.

Children and general use

The tools are general-purpose utilities and are not designed to collect personal information from children. Use the site responsibly and avoid entering confidential or sensitive data.

Contact

Before publishing, add a monitored contact email or another real contact method here and ensure the Contact page matches it.

This page is informational and is not legal advice. Review it against your actual services and applicable laws before applying to advertising programs.
