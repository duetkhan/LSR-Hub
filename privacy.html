Privacy Policy

How LSR Hub handles information when you use our website and tools.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

LSR Hub provides online tools and informational content. This Privacy Policy explains general information-handling practices associated with the website. Actual data handling may depend on the feature, browser, hosting provider and third-party services in use.

1. Information processed by our tools

Many LSR Hub tools are designed to process the text or files you provide directly in your browser. For these tools, input may be processed locally instead of being uploaded to an LSR Hub server. However, this may not apply to every feature or to services provided by third parties.

Your browser may make requests to our hosting provider or to external libraries and services used by particular features. Those providers may receive technical information, such as your IP address, browser details and request information, as part of delivering their services. Avoid entering passwords, payment details or highly sensitive information into online tools.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

The website or third-party services may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for functions such as remembering preferences, delivering services, measuring usage or displaying advertising. The technologies actually used may vary by page and service.

Your browser settings may allow you to review, restrict or delete cookies and site data. Restricting them may affect some website features.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

LSR Hub may use Google AdSense to display advertisements if the site is approved and advertising is enabled. When Google advertising services are active, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and policies.

Depending on your location and the applicable requirements, you may have choices about personalized advertising and the use of cookies or similar technologies. Where legally required, appropriate consent or privacy-choice mechanisms must be implemented.

Learn more at Google's Advertising Technologies and manage available advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center .

4. Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, message contents and any attachments. We use that information to respond to your enquiry, address feedback or investigate a reported issue.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information. Email communications may be retained in our email service for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry and maintain relevant records.

5. Third-party services

Our website may rely on hosting providers, content delivery networks, external libraries and advertising providers. These services may process technical information under their own privacy terms. LSR Hub does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers.

6. Data security and retention

We aim to use appropriate measures to protect information handled through the website. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information retention depends on the service involved and the purpose for which information was provided.

7. Children's privacy

LSR Hub provides general-purpose tools and informational content. The website is not intended to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service we control, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

8. Your choices and privacy questions

You can contact us with questions about this policy or a request concerning information you have sent directly to us. We will review requests and respond where reasonably possible and as required by applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, advertising services or data-handling practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
